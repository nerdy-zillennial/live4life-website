Support our work

We have paid for this ourselves. We would like to go further.

Everything since 2023 — the conferences, the parliament, the printed materials — came from our own pockets. Now there is something built, and ten more schools waiting.

What your giving pays for

  • A sitting. Hall, transport for delegates from three schools, refreshments, and the printed standing orders, ballot papers and handbooks a House needs to meet.
  • A new House. Establishing the Students’ Parliament in another secondary school: facilitator training, materials, and the first full day.
  • A Nation Building Conference. Bringing the programme onto a campus and putting undergraduates on their feet.
  • The coordinator we do not yet have. One full-time person, so that this stops depending on evenings and weekends.

We publish what we spend. Anyone who gives is told what it paid for.

Other ways to help

Money is not the only thing we are short of.

Open a school to us

If you lead or govern a secondary school in Plateau and want a House of your own, write to us. The design is finished and ready to hand over.

Host a conference

If you are at a tertiary institution and would host a Nation Building Conference on your campus, we will come to you.

Lend us what you know

We need people who understand governance, finance and safeguarding more than we need another supporter in name.

Start a conversation

We would rather show you something finished than ask you to fund a promise.
