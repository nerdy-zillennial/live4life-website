What your gift does

Where the money actually goes

A sitting

Put the House in a room for a day

Hall, transport for delegates from three schools, refreshments, and the printed standing orders, ballot papers and handbooks a House needs to meet.

A new House

Establish a parliament in another school

Facilitator training, materials, and the first full day. The design is finished and written to be handed over, so this is the cost of people and transport, not of invention.

A conference

Put a Nation Building Conference on a campus

Venue, logistics, materials and facilitation for one day, and the cohort of students that comes out of it.

A year

The coordinator we do not yet have

One full-time person, so that this stops depending on evenings and weekends. It is the single thing that would change our pace most.

Ask us what any of these costs and we will send you the figures from last year, itemised. We publish what we spend, and anyone who gives is told what it paid for.

Other ways to help

Not everything useful is money

  • Open a school to us. If you lead or govern a secondary school in Plateau and want a House of your own, write to us. The design is finished and ready to hand over.
  • Host a conference. If you are a tertiary institution, student body or faculty, invite us onto your campus and we will come to you.
  • Give a venue or a service. Space, printing, transport, catering and photography all cost us real money we would rather spend on young people.
  • Lend us what you know. We need people who understand governance, finance and safeguarding more than we need another supporter in name.
  • Open a door. An internship, an apprenticeship or a first job for a young person who is ready is worth more than most donations.
