About us

Mentorship is the whole method, not one of the things we offer

Live4Life Impact Crew Empowerment Foundation is a Nigerian not-for-profit organisation, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission in 2024 and based in Jos, Plateau State.

Knowledge can be handed over in an afternoon. Character cannot. The steadiness to make a hard decision, the judgement to know a bad offer when you see one, the confidence to speak in a room where you are the youngest person present: these are formed slowly, by someone who stays. That is what a mentor is for, and it is why everything else we do sits on top of a mentoring relationship rather than beside it.

We work in communities where a young person’s future is decided early and by other people. Our answer is not a course or a cheque. It is a relationship that lasts — and, increasingly, an institution they run themselves once the relationship has done its work.

Mission

To mentor young people in underserved communities into capable, self-sufficient adults and trustworthy leaders, able to shape their own futures and to improve the places they come from.

Vision

A generation of young Nigerians who were not left to work it out alone, and who are ready when responsibility finds them.

What we hold to

L.I.F.E.

LearningGrowth does not stop, for the mentee or the mentor. We teach, and we expect to be taught.
InclusivityA young person’s postcode, income or schooling should not decide how far they get. Too often it does. We work against that.
FellowshipPeople are carried by other people. We build the kind of community that is still there when the funding is not.
EmpowermentWe are not here to speak for young people. We are here until they can speak for themselves, and then we sit down.

How we got here

Four years, and what each one taught us

We did not set out to build a parliament. We arrived at one, by finding out what the other things could not do.

2022

A trade

Born out of LEAP Africa’s Youth Leadership Development Programme. A $250 grant funded skills workshops in Jos — soap, shoe making, baking — for young people at risk of drug abuse.

It taught us: a trade helps one young person. It does not change what they believe about their place in the country.

2023

A campaign

A $1,000 Nigeria Youth Futures Fund grant funded a campaign against voter apathy. Young people we worked with walked into INEC offices and collected their PVCs.

It taught us: you can move young people to act once. The apathy returns when there is nothing to belong to afterwards.

2024

A purpose

Registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission and rebranded. Everything narrowed to a single purpose: mentoring young leaders equipped to take on responsibility.

It taught us: we had been running events. What was missing was an institution students could hold on to.

2026

An institution

Three Nation Building Conferences in twelve months, and the Students’ Parliament launched on 7 November with forty-five students from three schools.

Our answer: a body the students run themselves, which meets again whether or not we are in the room.

Our mark

What the logo means

The arch is a mentor’s profile, drawn open rather than closed. Read around the curve, it carries learning and growth on one side, fellowship and community across the top, and empowerment on the other. Beneath it three circles rise in scale, each larger than the last: young Africans, thriving.

We chose an open arch deliberately. A mentor is a shelter, not a ceiling.

How we work

Three commitments

Quietly

We prepare young people for responsibilities that come to them later. We are not interested in putting children in front of a crowd now, and we publish no student’s name or photograph without written permission from a parent or guardian.

Non-political

We are registered as a not-for-profit, non-political organisation and our constitution requires us to stay that way. No party, no candidate, no campaign.

At our own cost

Everything since 2023 has been paid for by our own team. We would rather show you something finished than ask you to fund a promise.
