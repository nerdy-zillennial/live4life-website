About us

Mentorship is the method

Live4Life Impact Crew Empowerment Foundation is a Nigerian not-for-profit, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission and based in Jos, Plateau State.

Knowledge can be handed over in an afternoon. Character cannot. The steadiness to make a hard decision, the judgement to know a bad offer when you see one, the confidence to speak in a room where you are the youngest person present — these are formed slowly, by someone who stays.

We work where a young person’s future is decided early and by other people. Our answer is not a course or a cheque. It is a relationship that lasts.

Mission

To mentor young people in underserved communities into capable, self-sufficient adults and trustworthy leaders.

Vision

A generation of young Nigerians who were not left to work it out alone, and who are ready when responsibility finds them.

What we hold to

L.I.F.E.

LearningGrowth does not stop, for the mentee or the mentor.
InclusivityWhere a young person is from should not decide how far they get.
FellowshipPeople are carried by other people.
EmpowermentWe are here until they can speak for themselves, and then we sit down.

Our mark

A shelter, not a ceiling

The arch is a mentor’s profile, drawn open rather than closed. Around the curve it carries learning, fellowship and empowerment. Beneath it three circles rise in scale: African youths, thriving.

Quietly

We prepare young people for responsibilities that come to them later. We publish no young person’s name or photograph without written permission.

Non-political

Registered as a not-for-profit, non-political organisation, and our constitution requires us to stay that way.

At our own cost

We have funded this ourselves. We would rather show you something finished than ask you to fund a promise.
